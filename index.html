The business case

How much time is tied up?

The problem: Staff open documents, retype details, and repeat routine checks. Illustrative estimate—not a LambTech result: if each filing takes 10 minutes of manual work, 100 filings a month use about 17 staff hours. The solution: automation handles repeat steps and prepares a draft for a person to approve. Actual hours recovered depend on the process; measure a pilot before treating an estimate as ROI.
