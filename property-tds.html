Property TDS / Case study

How we automated 1% property TDS filing.

For a real estate client, we built a workflow that reads transaction documents, checks details, and prepares the filing for a person to review.

Client name withheld

The problem

Document checks. Details entered by hand.

The client's 1% property TDS filing involved checking transaction documents and moving details into the filing process manually. The work repeated from one transaction to the next.

We are not sharing the client's name or confidential business details.

What we built

A filing workflow with a human checkpoint.

The system handles repeatable document reading and checks, then prepares a draft. A person reviews and approves before the filing proceeds.

The workflow

Six steps. Review stays visible.

From the incoming documents to a recorded filing, each step has a clear place.

Human control

A draft is not the final decision.

A person checks the prepared filing before approval. The review is part of the workflow, not an afterthought.

What we can verify.

The intended time benefit is less repeat handling: the system reads details, checks rules, and prepares a draft for human verification.
